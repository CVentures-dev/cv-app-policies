# Pathfinder Sync – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

## Who we are

Pathfinder Sync is a Chrome extension used only by Contrarian Ventures team members. It copies the signed-in member's LinkedIn connections list into Contrarian Ventures' internal database, so the team can see who knows whom. It is not offered to the public.

The controller is Contrarian Ventures, Vingrių g. 3D-3, 01118 Vilnius, Lithuania. Contact: dev@cventures.vc.

## What we collect and why

| Whose data | What | Where it comes from | Why | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Team members using the extension | Work email, name, LinkedIn profile ID and handle, sync records (times, counts, errors) | Google sign-in and the member's LinkedIn session | Sign-in and running the sync | Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): operating our internal tools |
| People connected to a team member on LinkedIn | Name, LinkedIn profile ID and handle, headline, date connected to that team member | The team member's own LinkedIn connections list | Showing which team members know a person, for warm introductions in investing and fundraising | Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): professional networking |

We do not collect messages, emails, browsing history, passwords or LinkedIn cookies. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions about anyone.

## Storage, access and retention

- **Storage:** our database is hosted by Supabase in the EU (Ireland). Google provides sign-in. Both act as our processors under data processing agreements. If a provider accesses data from outside the EEA, it is protected by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
- **Access:** only Contrarian Ventures team members signed in with company accounts. We share data with no one else, unless the law requires it.
- **Retention:** we keep a member's connections while they use Pathfinder. We delete a member's connections and sync records within 30 days after they stop using Pathfinder or leave Contrarian Ventures, along with anyone no longer connected to a team member.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its use, to receive a copy, or object to its use, including if you are only in our database as someone's LinkedIn connection. Email dev@cventures.vc and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to Lithuania's State Data Protection Inspectorate ([vdai.lrv.lt](https://vdai.lrv.lt)).

Pathfinder Sync's use of data complies with the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the Limited Use requirements.

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date.
